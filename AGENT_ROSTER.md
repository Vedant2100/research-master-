## Assignment mechanics

The specialist profiles are configurations, not live workers. Only invoke agents for active workstreams, and provide the correct canonical source and authorized compute. Closed workstreams remain in the roster for historical traceability. Every handoff must include immutable source commits, commands, exact per-episode evidence, run IDs, matched comparisons, costs, caveats and blockers. A human/coordinator reviews cross-project changes; agents do not self-certify results.
